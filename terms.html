Terms

CritPass, by the studio at business@xirdie.com. Last updated 2026-10-31.

Your work

  • Upload only your own work: models and animations you made, or have the right to have reviewed.
  • Buyers must be 18 or older.

What a review is

  • A review is automated feedback written by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) against the challenge's published rubric, from snapshots your browser rendered. Topology counts are measured by code.
  • It is advisory. It is not accreditation, a qualification or a human critique, and it can be wrong.
  • Edge flow is noted, not scored, until we have tested it well enough to grade it.

Prices and what you buy

  • A single full review is $9 and runs once.
  • A pack is 5 prepaid reviews for $29: a fixed number of reviews with no cash value and no expiry. It is not a balance or a wallet and can't be exchanged for money.
  • The monthly plan is $19 a month for up to 60 reviews per billing period. It renews monthly until you cancel, and you can cancel any time from "Manage subscription" in the footer, which opens Stripe's billing portal (Stripe emails you a sign-in link).
  • The free quick review is one a day per connection and may be paused when our AI provider isn't answering.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars.

When a review fails

  • If a paid review fails on our side, you see your checkout id and can try again once. If it fails again, or you prefer, email business@xirdie.com with the id and we refund that review in full.
  • If the model declines to review your content, no grade is produced; we may decline to refund a review the model declined to grade.

No accounts

Your progress lives in your browser. Keep your restore code (shown after a pack or plan purchase) to use your purchase on another device; export your progress if you want a backup.

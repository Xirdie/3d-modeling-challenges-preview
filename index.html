FAQ

My .blend file won't load.

Browsers can't read .blend (or .ma/.max/.ztl). Export OBJ for modeling challenges (File > Export > Wavefront .obj, Triangulate off) or glTF; for animation, playblast to MP4 or a PNG sequence. Every challenge page has the steps for Blender, Maya and 3ds Max.

Why OBJ for modeling?

OBJ keeps your quads and n-gons, so we can count them. glTF and FBX arrive triangulated or vary by exporter; they still get the silhouette, proportion and brief grade, marked "topology not measured".

Is a person reviewing my work?

No. An AI model (Claude Sonnet 5.5 by Anthropic; Claude Haiku 5.5 for the quick review) grades the snapshots against the written rubric; topology counts are measured by code. Edge flow is noted but not scored yet. It is a fast first pass, not a human mentor.

What does the free quick review give me?

A grade band, two strengths and the biggest fix, from 3 low-res views, once a day. The full review grades every rubric line from 7 full-res views with steps to improve.

Is my model uploaded or stored?

No. The file stays in your browser; only rendered snapshots and your counts are sent for the review and none are kept.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

"Manage subscription" in the footer opens Stripe's billing portal (Stripe emails you a sign-in link); cancel there any time. Packs never expire. Paste your checkout id under "Restore purchase" to use a pack or plan on another device.

What if a review fails?

You'll see your checkout session id. Try again once, or email business@xirdie.com with the id and you get a full refund.

What it counts

  • Tris, quads and n-gons: faces with 3, 4, or 5 or more corners, read straight from the OBJ's f lines.
  • Poles: vertices where 3 edges meet, or 5 and more, counted apart and only away from open borders. A few are normal; clusters on curved surfaces cause pinching.
  • Non-manifold edges: edges shared by more than two faces, such as an extra face stuck inside the mesh. They break subdivision, booleans and 3D printing.
  • Open edges: edges with a face on one side only, the border of a hole.
  • Size: the bounding box in centimetres, with a metres or centimetres picker for your file's units.

Most web viewers triangulate a mesh before drawing it, so every quad shows a diagonal. This checker draws the polygon edges you modelled. If your counts show 0 quads and 0 n-gons, the file was exported triangulated: see how to export OBJ and keep your quads.
